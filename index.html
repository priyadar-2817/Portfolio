Details I sweat

Big decisions get the case studies. These are the small ones: states, feedback and the half-second in-betweens that make data-heavy B2B products feel calm. Click everything.

01 · Async actionclick

Buttons that tell the truth

Idle, working, done. The label changes with the state so nobody double-submits a sync.

02 · Progressclick

Progress you can trust

A sync that fails shows where it stopped, in plain words, not a spinner that spins forever.

03 · Loadingclick

Skeletons, not spinners

Skeletons hold the shape of the table, so the page doesn't jump when data lands.

04 · Validationtype

Errors that help

Inline, specific and kind. Say what's wrong and how to fix it, while the user is still looking.

We'll send sync events here.
05 · Reversibleclick

Undo beats “Are you sure?”

Confirm dialogs get clicked through on autopilot. An undo toast respects intent and fixes mistakes.

Alerts on · Undo
06 · Empty statelook

Empty states that teach

The first screen a new customer sees is usually empty. Use it to show the next step instead of a sad illustration.

About

I started out writing code, and got hooked on why people click what they click.

My first real project was an engineering internship at ServiceNow, building a sentiment model for Slack channels and incident surveys. That's where I got curious about how people actually behave inside products, which is a different question from how they say they behave.

Since then I've designed fintech infrastructure at KFin Technologies, HR software at SuperBeings and developer tools at Knit, where I'm the only designer. I run discovery through customer calls, Microsoft Clarity recordings and Heap funnels, then redesign against what people really do.
